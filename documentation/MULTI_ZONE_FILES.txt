📁 /ws/routes/ (created by setup script)

  Structure:
    /ws/routes/
    ├── {zone_from}_to_{zone_to}_{timestamp}.yaml  (route files)
    ├── zone_graph.yaml                             (auto-generated)
    └── examples/
        └── example_driveway.yaml                   (template)

  Route File Format (YAML):
    - route_id
    - route_name
    - from_zone_id / to_zone_id
    - route_type (DRIVEWAY, GATE_PASSAGE, etc.)
    - bidirectional (bool)
    - max_speed_mps (float)
    - path_width_meters (float)
    - mow_during_transit (bool)
    - waypoints[] (NavSatFix array)
    - total_distance_meters
    - estimated_transit_time_seconds
    - tags[] (string array)
    - created_at / last_validated (timestamps)

  Zone Graph Format (YAML):
    - nodes[] (ZoneGraphNode)
      - zone_id, zone_name
      - center_lat, center_lon
      - priority, last_mowed, estimated_time
    - edges[] (ZoneGraphEdge)
      - from_zone_id, to_zone_id
      - route_id, distance_meters
      - transit_time_seconds, bidirectional
