How to Record a Zone

  1. Enter a descriptive zone name
  2. Ensure GPS has good signal (green or yellow indicator)
  3. Click "Start Recording"
  4. Walk or drive the robot around the perimeter of the zone
  5. The system automatically samples waypoints every 0.5m
  6. Use "Pause" if you need to navigate around obstacles
  7. Click "Stop & Save" to finish and save the zone
