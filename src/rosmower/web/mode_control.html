Blade Motor PWM

Draft: 1000 us (stopped) · Applied: waiting for ROS
Servo 1 is used as the blade motor PWM output. `1000` stops the blade, `1500` is the minimum spinning speed, startup sends a quick `2000` pop before ramping, and the blade is blocked whenever height is fully high.
